# Muhammad Umair Sarwar

Mathematician working in machine learning and data analytics, based in Darmstadt, Germany.

- M.Sc. Mathematics (Mathematics in Data Science) at TU Darmstadt, and student research assistant
- M.Phil. Mathematics, Quaid-i-Azam University
- Interests: robust and reliable machine learning, uncertainty quantification, optimisation and game theory, reproducible data pipelines

## Machine learning and research projects

- [robust-federated-learning-ml-security](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/robust-federated-learning-ml-security): PyTorch study of federated learning under poisoning and backdoor attacks, with robust and fuzzy client-reliability aggregation.
- [strategic-classification-toy](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/strategic-classification-toy): simulation of strategic behaviour in classification, at the boundary of economics and machine learning.
- [germany-heat-risk-forecasting](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/germany-heat-risk-forecasting): next-day heat-risk forecasting for Darmstadt from DWD observations, with split-conformal prediction intervals.

## Data analytics and engineering projects

- [german-energy-operations](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/german-energy-operations): German electricity analytics with Python, SQL, dbt and DuckDB, including tested pipelines and a Streamlit dashboard.
- [retail-customer-analytics](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/retail-customer-analytics): retail sales and customer analytics with Python and SQL, including RFM segmentation and an executive report.
- [customer-churn-prediction](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/customer-churn-prediction): customer churn prediction with pandas and scikit-learn.
