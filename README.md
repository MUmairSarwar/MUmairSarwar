## Machine learning and research projects

- [fair-multiagent-battery-control](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/fair-multiagent-battery-control): multi-agent RL for six home batteries behind one transformer (PettingZoo; Q-learning, PPO, MAPPO vs. a perfect-foresight LP). PPO reached about 68% of the LP optimum and cut overload energy from 939 kWh to 58 kWh.
- [robust-federated-learning-ml-security](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/robust-federated-learning-ml-security): PyTorch study of federated learning under label poisoning and backdoor attacks, comparing FedAvg, median, trimmed mean and a fuzzy client-reliability rule.
- [germany-heat-risk-forecasting](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/germany-heat-risk-forecasting): next-day heat-risk forecasting for Darmstadt from DWD data, with split-conformal prediction intervals (71.1% coverage vs. an 80% target, reported openly).
- [strategic-classification](https://github.com/MUmairSarwar/strategic-classification): simulation of how a fixed classifier changes when people adjust a feature to get accepted.

## Data analytics and engineering projects
